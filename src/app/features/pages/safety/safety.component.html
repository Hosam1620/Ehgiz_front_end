Safety Guide

Your safety is our top priority. Follow these guidelines for a safe and smooth rental experience.

Tips for Renters

  • Check reviews before booking

    Read reviews from previous renters to confirm the tool is in good condition and the owner is reliable.

  • Inspect the tool at pickup

    Document any existing damage with photos before you use the tool to protect yourself from false damage claims.

  • Communicate through the platform

    Always message owners through Ehgiz, never via personal contact, so conversations are recorded for dispute resolution.

  • Pay through the platform only

    Never pay outside of Ehgiz. Off-platform payments are not covered by our buyer protection or insurance policy.

Tips for Tool Owners

  • Verify renter profiles

    Check the renter's review history and account age before confirming a booking request.

  • Document tool condition

    Take clear photos of your tool before each rental and keep records of its condition to support any insurance claims.

  • Provide clear usage instructions

    Include safety guidelines and proper usage instructions with your listing to prevent misuse and accidents.

Report a safety issue

If you experience any safety concerns, damage, or suspicious behaviour, contact our support team immediately.
