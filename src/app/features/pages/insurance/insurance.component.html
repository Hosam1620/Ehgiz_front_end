Insurance Coverage

Every rental on Ehgiz includes automatic insurance coverage so both renters and owners are protected.

Accidental Damage

Coverage up to 5,000 EGP per rental for accidental damage caused by the renter.

Theft Protection

In verified cases of theft, owners receive compensation up to the declared tool value.

No Extra Cost

Insurance is included automatically in every booking, no separate policy needed.

How to file a claim

  1. Document the damage: take photos immediately when you discover the issue.
  2. Report within 24 hours: contact our support team within 24 hours of the rental end date.
  3. Submit evidence: provide photos, booking details, and a description of what happened.
  4. Review process: our team will review the claim within 3–5 business days and contact both parties.

What is not covered

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Damage reported after the 24-hour window
  • Off-platform rentals or payments
  • Pre-existing damage not documented before the rental
  • Intentional damage or misuse
