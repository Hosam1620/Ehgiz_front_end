What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

How Ehgiz uses cookies

Essential cookies Always on

Required for the platform to function. These handle authentication, security, and basic navigation. They cannot be disabled.

Preference cookies Optional

Remember your settings such as language, search filters, and display preferences to personalise your experience.

Analytics cookies Optional

Help us understand how users interact with the platform so we can improve features and fix issues. Data is aggregated and anonymous.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the platform. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Block all or certain cookies
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser

Third-party cookies

Some features of Ehgiz may use third-party services (such as payment processors) that set their own cookies. These are governed by the respective third-party's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the platform evolves. Changes will be reflected with an updated date above.
