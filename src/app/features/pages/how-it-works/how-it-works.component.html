How Ehgiz Works

Egypt's trusted peer-to-peer tool rental platform — rent the tools you need, or earn from the ones you own.

1

Browse Available Tools

Search through hundreds of tools listed by people near you. Filter by category, location, price, and availability.

2

Send a Booking Request

Select your rental dates and submit a request. Message the owner directly with any questions before confirming.

3

Pay Securely

Once the owner confirms, pay through our secure platform. Your payment is held safely until the rental is complete.

4

Return & Review

Return the tool at the end of your rental period, then leave a review to help the community. Payment is released to the owner.

Earn from your tools

Have tools sitting unused in your garage? List them on Ehgiz and earn money every time someone rents them. You set the price, availability, and terms.
